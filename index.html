Signature Crispy Fried Chicken & Craft Recipes

Our signature crispy fried chicken is freshly marinated with premium Himalayan spices, hand-breaded in our proprietary crunch coating, and pressure-fried to golden perfection to seal in natural tenderness and flavor. Savor our handcrafted burgers, including the best-selling Crunchy Fried Chicken Burger, juicy Chicken Burgers, and crispy Aloo Tikki Burgers. Layered with farm-fresh local lettuce, ripe tomatoes, caramelized onions, and house-made signature sauces inside warm toasted buns, Crunchy Bag delivers the best fast food burger experience in Nepal.

Authentic Nepalese Pakodas & Crispy Snacks

Indulge in authentic local favorites and crunchy fritters, including Paneer Pakoda, Gobi Pakoda, Onion Pakoda, Green Chicken Pakoda, and classic mixed vegetable pakodas served with tangy mint and coriander chutneys. Complete your feast with salted golden French fries and herb-seasoned crispy potatoes.

Chilled Beverages, Thick Shakes & Cold Coffee

Quench your thirst with our handcrafted drinks lineup, featuring rich Cold Coffee blended with Oreo cookies, thick chocolate milkshakes, traditional sweet yogurt Lassi, fresh seasonal fruit juices, and chilled Coke.
